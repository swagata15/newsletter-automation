# Research notes: AI relationships and love
Read 2026-11-05.

## [1] Match & The Kinsey Institute (2025), 14th annual Singles in America study
URL (report): https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/a-funny-bone-to-pick/202506/ai-use-in-dating-jumps-333
- "Funded by Match and conducted by Dynata in association with The Kinsey Institute, the Singles in America study surveyed a demographically representative sample of 5,001 single folks in the U.S."
- "16% of singles have engaged with AI as a romantic companion. This percentage was highest among Gen Zers, at 33%, followed by Millennials at 23%."
- "those actively dating humans were nearly three times more likely than inactive daters (23% versus 8%) to turn to AI for companionship."
- "Forty-five percent reported that AI partners made them feel more understood, and 44% said that AI offered stronger emotional support." (i.e. 45% felt more understood; 44% stronger emotional support)
- Cheating: "40% felt that having an AI boyfriend/girlfriend while already in an exclusive relationship with a human would be considered cheating, 39% indicated that sexual chats with AI would be a no-no, and 38% said the same about emotionally intimate conversations with AI." "41% believed that none of those activities would be considered cheating."

## [2] Zhang, Zhao, Hancock, Kraut & Yang (2025), The Rise of AI Companions: Interaction with AI Companions and Psychological Well-being (Stanford / Carnegie Mellon)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12605
- 1,131 U.S. adults who use Character.AI; 4,664 chat sessions (464,687 messages) from 237 participants.
- "Smaller social networks were associated with reporting companionship as the primary chatbot use ... which in turn was associated with lower well-being"
- The association was stronger when interactions were intensive and highly disclosive.
- "the association between AI companionship and well-being is not uniform and depends on how chatbots are used and users' offline social environments."

## [3] Yuan, Zhang, Aledavood, Zhang & Saha (2025), Mental Health Impacts of AI Companions (Aalto University)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22505 ; press release https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1122239
- Reddit activity of nearly 2,000 active Replika users, one year before and after first mentioning the AI companion, compared with matched users; plus 18 interviews.
- "greater grief expression and interpersonal focus, alongside increases in language about loneliness, depression, and suicidal ideation."
- AI companions "provided emotional validation and social rehearsal but also carried risks of over-reliance and withdrawal."
- Talayeh Aledavood: "AI companions offer unconditional and unflagging support – something that's very attractive to people who are struggling socially. But it also quietly raises the perceived cost of human relationships, which are messy, unpredictable, and require effort," ... "Over time, people stop reaching out."

## [4] Pang, Gao, Wang & Hui (2026), The AI Amplifier Effect: Defining Human-AI Intimacy and Romantic Relationships with Conversational AI
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08084
- In-depth interviews with 30 users in romantic relationships with AI companions.
- "the AI Amplifier Effect, where the AI serves as a medium that intensifies the user's existing emotional state, leading to divergent positive, neutral, and negative impacts."
